Content packs

The digest is the SHA-256 of the pack's canonical parse — it covers content, not formatting, so re-indenting a pack does not change it. Signing is HMAC-SHA256 with the shared CONTENT_PACK_KEY: it proves a pack came from a holder of that key, which is the model for an internal or air-gapped pack repository. For a third-party pack, compare this digest against a detached signature published out of band.
